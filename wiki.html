Precognition (MN)

Years active2025–present

Websiteofficialprecognition.com

Precognition is a music duo from Minnesota founded by Jacob Alexander Figueroa, known professionally as Zander. The band describes its sound as “sound before impact,” blending emotion, chaos, and instinct into genre-defying compositions.

Concept and Style

The name Precognition refers to the ability to sense future events. Their music is rooted in trauma, truth, and transformation, often incorporating ambient textures, metal riffs, and experimental production.
