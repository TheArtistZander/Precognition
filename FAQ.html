What Is Precognition?

Precognition is the ability to perceive or sense future events before they happen. At Precognition, we channel this concept into music—transforming emotion, instinct, and foresight into immersive soundscapes.

Meet the Artists

Sam and Zander are the minds behind Precognition. Their work blends intuitive intelligence with sonic storytelling, creating tracks that feel like glimpses into tomorrow.

Interview with Sam & Zander

Q: What inspired the name “Precognition”?

Sam: We wanted something that captured the feeling of knowing before knowing. Music, for us, is a form of foresight—an emotional forecast.

Q: How do you translate intuition into sound?

Zander: We start with silence and let instinct guide the rhythm. Every track is a conversation between what we feel and what we fear.

Q: What do you hope listeners take away from your music?

Sam: A sense of clarity. Maybe even a glimpse of their own future.

Q: Is Precognition a genre or a movement?

Zander: It’s both. It’s a genre of feeling and a movement of emotional honesty.
