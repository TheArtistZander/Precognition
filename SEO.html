Why Listen to Precognition?

Precognition blends haunting synth textures with raw guitar energy, creating a sound that's both futuristic and deeply emotional. If you're into bands like Radiohead, Nine Inch Nails, or Depeche Mode, you'll feel right at home.

Our music explores themes of memory, identity, and the surreal—perfect for late-night drives or introspective moments.
