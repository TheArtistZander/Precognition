🔮 August 2025 — The Signal Begins

Precognition is more than a band—it’s a transmission. Sam and I have been crafting this project in silence for months, letting instinct guide the sound. It’s raw, cinematic, and unapologetically emotional. We’re not chasing trends. We’re chasing truth.

Our first release is coming soon. It’s called Echoes Before Impact, and it’s exactly what it sounds like—a warning, a memory, a prophecy. Stay tuned.

🧬 July 2025 — Why Precognition?

I joined Precognition because I felt something real. Zander’s vision is magnetic, and together we’re building something that feels alive. I’ve never worked on music this intuitive before—it’s like the songs write themselves. I’m excited to share what we’ve made.

🎛️ June 2025 — Studio Rituals

Our studio isn’t just a place—it’s a ritual space. We light candles, we paint, we record in bursts. Sometimes we don’t speak for hours. Sometimes we scream into the mic and layer it with synths. Precognition is chaos sharpened by instinct.
